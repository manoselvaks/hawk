Published on  23 March 2023

Monetary Policy Summary, March 2023

The Bank of England's Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) sets monetary policy to meet the 2% inflation target. At its meeting ending on 22 March 2023, the MPC voted by a majority of 7–2 to increase Bank Rate by 0.25 percentage points, to 4.25%.

The MPC will continue to monitor closely indications of persistent inflationary pressures.
