Inflation and the path of Bank Rate - speech by Andrew Bailey

Published on  14 March 2023
Published on  14 March 2023

Andrew Bailey

Governor

Speech given by Andrew Bailey, Governor of the Bank of England, at the London School of Economics on Monday 13 March 2023.

Inflation remains far too high. The Monetary Policy Committee will do what is necessary to return inflation to the 2% target sustainably in the medium term.

If there were evidence of more persistent pressures, further tightening in monetary policy would be required.
