How to label

For each document, read the passages and decide which way the author leans on the future path of UK interest rates, compared with policy at the time. Judge the lean, not the level of rates. Aim for under two minutes per item; open the full text only if the passages are not enough.

−2Very dovish. “The case for a further cut in Bank Rate is now compelling.” −1Dovish. “With demand weak, there is no need to tighten any time soon.” 0Neutral. “Risks to inflation are broadly balanced.” +1Hawkish. “I am watching wage growth closely; upside risks have grown.” +2Very hawkish. “Bank Rate needs to rise, and soon, to bring inflation back to target.”

Choose Not about monetary policy when the document is about something else (financial stability, regulation, payments, banknotes, climate, the Bank itself). Add a note if an item was hard to call.
