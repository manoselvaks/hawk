About

A markets question, answered in the open.

Central banks talk before they act. Hawk measures that talk for the Bank of England over almost three decades and asks whether it carried information that traders and economists could have used, without the benefit of hindsight.

The project

Hawk collects every Bank of England speech and every set of Monetary Policy Committee minutes since 1997, together with Bank Rate decisions, members’ votes and gilt yields. Each document is scored from dovish to hawkish by a language model and by a transparent word-list baseline, and both are checked against hand labels before any result is reported.

The code, data and every decision along the way are public. If you spot a mistake, please open an issue.
