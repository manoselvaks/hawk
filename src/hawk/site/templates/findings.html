One labeller, about 36 labels

The AI scorer is checked against one person’s hand labels. That separates a useful scorer from a useless one, but the confidence intervals are wide.

The model knows history

The language model was trained on text written after most of these speeches. Hiding the date and speaker reduces, but cannot remove, the risk that it recalls what happened next.

Correlation, not causation

Tone and decisions are both driven by the economic data. A link between them does not mean speeches caused the moves.
